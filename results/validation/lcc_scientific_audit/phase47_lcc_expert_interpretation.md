# Post-Ranking Expert Shortlist: LCC Engineering Candidates (Phase 47)

*This shortlist represents a post-hoc scientific interpretation of the Function-First Top30 for LCC. It integrates structural mechanism, transfer confidence, evolutionary variability, and literature evidence. It does NOT modify the frozen Function-First ranking.*

---

## 1. Top 5 Strongest Experimental Candidates for Initial Testing
1. **LCC H184 (Ref S214) — Target Mutation: `H184Y`**
   - **Top30 Rank**: `#8`
   - **Transfer Confidence**: `HIGH CONFIDENCE`
   - **Rationale**: Mapped to the cleft rim surface loop. `H184Y` is the **#1 Evolutionary WHAT proposal** (115 Atlas MSA sequences) and directly reproduces the validated literature benchmark mutation `LCC H218Y` (Cribari et al. 2023, Orr et al. 2024).
2. **LCC F208 (Ref F238) — Target Mutation: `F208I` / `F208L`**
   - **Top30 Rank**: `#3`
   - **Transfer Confidence**: `HIGH CONFIDENCE`
   - **Rationale**: Direct cleft gate lining position (substrate distance 3.82 Å). `F208I` is a top evolutionary proposal that directly matches the published benchmark mutation `LCC F243I` (Cui et al. 2021).
3. **LCC Y59 (Ref Y87) — Target Mutation: `Y59F` / `Y59W`**
   - **Top30 Rank**: `#5`
   - **Transfer Confidence**: `HIGH CONFIDENCE`
   - **Rationale**: Key substrate aromatic subsite (substrate distance 4.21 Å). `Y59F` (142 sequences) and `Y59W` (48 sequences) modulate aromatic stacking and hydrogen bonding.
4. **LCC L153 (Ref I183) — Target Mutation: `L153V` / `L153I`**
   - **Top30 Rank**: `#4`
   - **Transfer Confidence**: `HIGH CONFIDENCE`
   - **Rationale**: Backing hydrophobic packing shell behind catalytic triad (catalytic distance 4.88 Å). `L153V` (112 sequences) adjusts packing volume to enhance thermostability.
5. **LCC M131 (Ref M161) — Target Mutation: `M131L` / `M131F`**
   - **Top30 Rank**: `#9`
   - **Transfer Confidence**: `HIGH CONFIDENCE`
   - **Rationale**: Active-site binding pocket methionine (catalytic distance 3.90 Å). `M131L` (110 sequences) and `M131F` (65 sequences) alter subsite volume and aromatic contact.

## 3. Caution / High-Risk Candidates List
- **LCC G128 (Ref G158, Rank #7)**: Hinge Glycine adjacent to catalytic S160/W159. Proposals like `G128A` risk rigidifying the catalytic loop and abolishing activity.
- **LCC A179 (Ref S209, Rank #1) — Proposal `A179C`**: Introducing an unpaired Cysteine near the active site poses a strong risk of aberrant disulfide formation and misfolding.
